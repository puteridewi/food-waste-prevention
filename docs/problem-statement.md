# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Households, families, and individual consumers who struggle with tracking perishable food items and meal planning.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Traditional grocery storage and manual tracking rely on static expiration dates and guesswork, failing to account for actual storage conditions, consumption habits, and inventory age, which results in forgotten food and frequent spoilage.

## Why does it matter?
These inefficiencies lead to unnecessary household financial losses, wasted grocery budgets, and a larger environmental footprints from discarded edible food.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Households and individual consumers are significantly impacted by the lack of proactive tools for tracking perishable groceries and predicting spoilage risks. Because conventional kitchen management relies heavily on guesswork and static expiry dates rather than dynamic freshness tracking, food is frequently forgotten and allowed to spoil. This ongoing issue creates unnecessary financial strain on household budgets and contributes heavily to domestic food waste. Implementing a machine learning-based food waste prevention system will enable accurate spoilage forecasting and intelligent inventory prioritization, helping households consume food before it goes to waste.
